description: Limits on legal claims about TimeNation's internal credit feature
---

Do not claim that the Time Bank is lawful in every country or that it violates no laws worldwide. The code confirms credit is issued in DarMin, tracked as debt, and repaid from balance or earned TiMETR minutes; it does not establish regulatory classification or legal compliance.

**Why:** consumer-credit and financial-service rules differ by jurisdiction, and a global legal conclusion cannot be established from product code or a general web review.

**How to apply:** Keep user-facing wording factual and state that legal status requires qualified review in each target jurisdiction. Do not present a disclaimer as proof of compliance.
