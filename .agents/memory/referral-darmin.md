name: DarMin and ancestral-minute distinction
description: Citizenship grants one non-transferable ancestral minute valued at 1 Darmin; referral DarMin is separate.
---

When citizenship is granted, the citizen's account receives one non-transferable ancestral minute. Its stated value is 1 Darmin, the in-game currency of virtual TimeNation; do not describe this as the citizen receiving 1 Darmin currency as the minute itself. A referrer receives 15 Darmin only after the invited citizen's successful passport issuance. Referral bonuses are a separate restricted balance, may pay for in-network services and goods, and must remain restricted when a citizen receives them as payment. They must never enter the ordinary balance used by payment gateways or exchange-like flows.

**Why:** the user clarified that the ancestral minute is a non-transferable citizenship grant valued at 1 Darmin, not the same thing as Darmin currency; referral rewards are separate.

**How to apply:** Registration copy must distinguish the non-transferable ancestral-minute grant from Darmin currency. Any new citizen-to-citizen service or marketplace settlement must keep cash and referral-credit portions separate in escrow, taxes, refunds, and recipient credits. External payment and wallet flows should use only the ordinary balance. Shareable referral links should start at the published web gate `/web/welcome?ref=...`; the gate must preserve the attribution through registration.
