---
name: Referral DarMin restrictions
description: TimeNation referral attribution, issuance rewards, and non-cashable bonus balance policy.
---

New citizens receive exactly one ancestral DarMin when their passport is issued. A referrer receives 15 DarMin only after that successful issuance. Referral bonuses are a separate restricted balance, may pay for in-network services and goods, and must remain restricted when a citizen receives them as payment. They must never enter the ordinary balance used by payment gateways or exchange-like flows.

**Why:** the user explicitly requires a 1 DarMin starting balance, a 15 DarMin referral reward after successful registration/issuance, and technical prevention of cash-out.

**How to apply:** Any new citizen-to-citizen service or marketplace settlement must keep cash and referral-credit portions separate in escrow, taxes, refunds, and recipient credits. External payment and wallet flows should use only the ordinary balance.
