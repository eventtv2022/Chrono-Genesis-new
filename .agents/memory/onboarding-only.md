---
name: Tutorial-only onboarding
description: Product boundary for the TimeNation onboarding tutorial.
---

The onboarding tutorial is educational only. Completing it must not issue a passport, change citizenship status, or alter a citizen's DarMin balance. Passport issuance remains a separate existing process.

**Why:** the user explicitly chose “Только обучение” and asked to preserve the existing passport process.

**How to apply:** Keep tutorial routes and completion writes limited to onboarding state; do not trigger passport issuance, citizenship transitions, transfers, rewards, or balance updates.
