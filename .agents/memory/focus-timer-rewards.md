---
name: Focus timer rewards
description: Product rules for focus-session and TimeNation quiz rewards
---

A completed focus session of at least 12 minutes earns 1 Darmin per 1,800 characters typed, counting spaces. Separately, every 12 correct TimeNation quiz answers earn 1 Darmin.

**Why:** the user requested both reward mechanics on 2026-10-23.

**How to apply:** Keep the text transient (do not persist the citizen's thoughts); calculate the reward from server-measured elapsed time and submitted text. Quiz progress accumulates for the citizen across attempts.
