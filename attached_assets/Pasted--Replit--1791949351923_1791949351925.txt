В предыдущем варианте промта для Replit акцент был сделан в основном на ограничениях и блокировках для обеспечения юридической безопасности.

Давайте скорректируем промт так, чтобы **образовательная, философская и игровая ценность** концепции «Время как деньги» для подростков (13–17 лет) была прописана как полноценный режим (*Sandbox / Educational Mode*), а не просто как запрет.

Вот обновленный и сбалансированный вариант промта, который дает подросткам возможность развивать мышление нового поколения, оставаясь в рамках закона:

```text
Implement International Child Safety, Age-Gate, and Educational Sandbox Mode for TimeNation.

Objective:
Comply with global privacy regulations (COPPA, GDPR-K) while preserving a rich educational, philosophical, and gamified experience for teenagers (ages 13-17) around the concept of "Time as Currency" and historical timelines.

Requirements & Age Tiers:

1. Age-Gate Onboarding Flow:
   - During signup/registration, prompt the user for their Date of Birth (DOB).
   - Automatically assign the user profile to one of three age tiers:
     a) Under 13 (Strict Block / COPPA compliance)
     b) 13 to 17 (Teen / Educational Sandbox Mode)
     c) 18+ (Full Access / Adult & Web3)

2. Feature Access Logic & Philosophy per Tier:

   [Tier A: Under 13 Years Old]
   - Block account creation or require Verifiable Parental Consent (VPC). Do not collect precise personal data or device tracking IDs.

   [Tier B: 13 – 17 Years Old (Educational Sandbox & Philosophy Mode)]
   - PHILOSOPHY: Empower the new generation to understand the value of time, history, and strategic resource allocation through safe gamification.
   - ALLOW: 
     * Full exploration of the history timeline and interactive educational cards.
     * Participation in an internal non-financial sandbox economy (earning and spending internal "Time Credits" or educational points through learning activities and historical quests).
     * Simulating resource management and time-investment strategies without real financial risk.
   - RESTRICT / DISABLE:
     * Real-world financial transactions, fiat payment gateways (Freedom Bank APIs), and subscription checkouts.
     * Web3 wallet integrations, token/NFT purchases, and DAO voting weight.
     * Unmoderated public user-generated content (UGC) publishing.
   - UI / UX Banner: Display an inspiring indicator: "TimeNation Sandbox: Explore time, history, and value. Real-world financial features unlock at 18."

   [Tier C: 18+ Years Old (Full Access)]
   - Unlock full ecosystem: Web3, DAO governance, Freedom Bank e-Residency integration, real estate SPV modules, and real-world asset tokenization upon KYC completion.

3. Backend & Database Enforcement:
   - Store user age tier on the User model (`ageTier: 'UNDER_13' | 'TEEN' | 'ADULT'`).
   - Secure all financial and Web3 API endpoints with server-side middleware, returning a `403 Forbidden` for any non-ADULT requests trying to access real transactions.

```

Такой подход защищает проект юридически, но при этом сохраняет глубокую миссию проекта — обучать молодое поколение новому мышлению через игру с историей и временем.
