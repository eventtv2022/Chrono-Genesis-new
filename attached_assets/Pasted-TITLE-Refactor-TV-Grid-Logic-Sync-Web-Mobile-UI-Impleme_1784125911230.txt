TITLE: Refactor TV Grid Logic: Sync Web/Mobile UI, Implement Correct Slot Pricing, and Fix Color States

1. Slot Tier & Pricing Structure (Exclude 2% tax in UI display, calculate at checkout)
TV slots are priced based on their time position within the hour. Update the legend and database pricing to reflect these three distinct tiers:
- "Четверть / Без четверти" (Quarter past / Quarter to, e.g., XX:15, XX:45): 4.99 € or 5 Darmin (Д)

- "Ровно" (Top of the hour, e.g., XX:00): 24.99 € or 25 Darmin (Д)
* Note: All prices displayed in the legend/slots are net (excluding a 2% transaction tax which is added at checkout).

2. Unified Color States (Must be identical on Web and Mobile)

- EMPTY / FREE (Dark Gray/Black): Available slot.

- GREEN (First Tap / Selection): When a user taps/clicks any free slot, it immediately turns Green (representing "active selection" before payment).
- YELLOW (Booked & Paid): Once a slot is successfully booked and paid for, its square turns Yellow.
- ORANGE (On Air / Archive / Scheduled): Slots that are currently broadcasting, have already aired, or are locked by system scheduling turn Orange.
